### Alpahaar Foods 

So the idea is we will build a web app for my Mom, to help her generating messages for her food business which she shares the message in her marketing channels like whatsapp groups to promote that days menu to attract customers, should be very simple and will be using AI to generate those messages based on her selection,

So typical scenario is lets say today is 4th October, Saturday, so usually she drops a messages in a foodies whatsapp group, where all the foodie people are there for our society, Usually the message will be dropped on saturday so that by Sunday 11am people will order those food items through messaging us on whatsapp and paying. So usually we make Samosas, Cutlets, Aloo chops and Bread Pakodas on Sunday, but our menu keeps changing every weekend, sometimes we add packaged mixtures,sev and chiwda, or sweets too like bundiya, or balusai , so the menus is not fixed and then deliver them for the orders by 6pm on Sunday Evening to the customers, and the amount she cooks food depends on the order that is received from the customers.
